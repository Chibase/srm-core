## 1. Purpose

TrustLedger exists to help communities and implementing entities understand whether a project is building genuine trust, whether participation is voluntary and meaningful, and whether the evidence shows trust is growing or declining.

The product must be built for Global South realities and must be able to prove community participation, trust movement, and the evidence behind those claims.

---

## 2. Product promise

TrustLedger should be able to answer:
- Do communities trust the project?
- Do they trust the implementing entity, the process, and the people?
- Are they participating because they want to, not only because they gain something?
- Is trust increasing or declining?
- What evidence proves it?
- What should be done next to build trust?

If the platform cannot answer these questions, it is not fulfilling its purpose.

---

## 3. Build philosophy

### 3.1 Evolve, do not disrupt
The platform must be extended safely. Existing users must be protected.

### 3.2 Enhance before replace
Current modules must be reused where possible before creating new ones.

### 3.3 Add trust in parallel
Trust-native structures should be built alongside legacy structures before any cutover.

### 3.4 Evidence must back all claims
No trust claim is valid without traceable evidence.

### 3.5 Global South first
Every product decision must be tested against real community and field conditions.

### 3.6 AI must remain explainable
AI supports trust intelligence; it does not replace evidence or judgment.

## 5. How the current platform contributes

### Measurement and outcomes
Current measurement structures become the base for trust indicators and trust change tracking.

### Evidence
Current evidence design becomes the proof layer for trust, participation, and context claims.

### Stakeholder response
Current response and sentiment capture becomes the source of community feeling, trust barriers, and willingness data.

### Segmentation
Current geographic and stakeholder segmentation becomes trust-by-community and trust-by-location analysis.

### AI methods
Current AI stubs become trust triage, trust summary, and trust response support.

### Ops and probes
Current health/readiness and hardening measures protect the reliability of the platform while it grows.

---

## 6. Implementation sequence

The build must follow this sequence:
1. stabilize the current state,
2. enhance existing modules,
3. add trust-native structures in parallel,
4. build trust analytics,
5. build trust intelligence and recommendations,
6. adapt for Global South reality,
7. package MVP.

No later stage should begin before the prior stage has passed its gate.

---

## 7. Safety rules

- Do not break current user workflows unnecessarily.
- Do not delete or rename legacy structures early.
- Do not make new trust logic mandatory before it is validated.
- Do not replace current reports until trust-report equivalents are ready.
- Do not allow AI outputs to become untraceable or opaque.

---

## 8. MVP target

The MVP is reached when the platform can:
- measure trust,
- measure willingness to participate,
- attach evidence to trust claims,
- show trust trends,
- recommend trust-building actions,
- and produce proof-ready reports.

---

## 9. Governance for implementation work

Every implementation batch must answer:
- Does this protect current users?
- Does this move the platform closer to trust measurement and proof?
- Does this remain explainable and evidence-backed?
- Does this work in Global South contexts?

If the answer is no, the batch must be revised before implementation.

---

## 10. Final statement

TrustLedger is not just an SRM tool.
It is a trust evidence and trust intelligence platform for community-facing work.

The platform must be built so that trust can be measured, explained, defended, and improved — without disturbing the continuity of the current system.
