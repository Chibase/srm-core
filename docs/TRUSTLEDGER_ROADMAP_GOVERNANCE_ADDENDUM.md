# TrustLedger Roadmap Governance Addendum

> Companion governance note for `docs/MASTER_ROADMAP_ALL_PHASES.md`.
>
> This addendum clarifies that the TrustLedger roadmap must be executed in a safe, additive, continuity-preserving manner that protects current users while progressively repurposing the platform toward the trust-centered MVP.

---

## 1. Relationship to the roadmap

The roadmap defines the product trajectory.
This addendum defines the **rules of movement** along that trajectory.

The roadmap must be interpreted through these constraints:
- do not break current workflows without a migration plan,
- do not replace core modules prematurely,
- do not remove legacy outputs until trust-layer replacements are validated,
- and do not advance to later phases without passing earlier gates.

---

## 2. Roadmap execution policy

### Policy A: Enhance before replace
Prefer extending current modules before introducing new ones.

### Policy B: Parallel build before cutover
Where a new trust-native structure is needed, build it beside the current module first.

### Policy C: Validate before adoption
No trust metric, recommendation engine, or reporting path becomes authoritative until validated against evidence and user needs.

### Policy D: Preserve continuity
Legacy API shapes, reports, and workflows must remain available through transition periods.

---

## 3. Authority chain

From highest to lowest:
1. TrustLedger Platform Blueprint
2. TrustLedger Safe Implementation Process
3. TrustLedger Master Roadmap
4. TrustLedger Engineering Document Series
5. Build plan / packets / implementation notes

If there is a conflict, the higher authority governs the lower one.

---

## 4. Required checkpoints before each phase move

Before moving to the next phase, confirm:
- current users are protected,
- the prior phase is stable,
- data model changes are additive or reversible,
- evidence trails remain intact,
- and the new layer improves MVP readiness.

---

## 5. Non-negotiables for MVP direction

The roadmap must continue to support:
- trust as a measurable outcome,
- community participation as a trust signal,
- evidence-backed proof generation,
- Global South operating realities,
- and AI assistance that remains explainable and auditable.

---

## 6. Adoption rule

No team should treat the roadmap as a speculative wish list.
It is the guide for implementation, sequencing, and MVP realization.

Any proposed deviation must be reviewed against:
- user safety,
- continuity,
- trust mission fit,
- and Global South relevance.
